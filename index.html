Skill Training Advisor

An Alt1 app for RuneScape 3. Pick a skill and a goal — fastest XP, AFK, or profit — and it lays out the wiki's training methods for your level: the gear worth bringing, the unlocks you're missing, and every training spot on a map with the game's teleport network overlaid, so the quickest way there is obvious. Reads all 29 of your levels straight off the skills panel.

DailyScape Advisor

An Alt1 checklist for RuneScape 3's dailies, weeklies and monthlies — rebuilt for the game after the March 2026 DailyScape Overhaul. Checkboxes reset themselves on game time, and typing your RSN filters the list to what your account can actually do (quests via RuneMetrics, levels via hiscores). Each task carries its requirements, tips, a map pin with the nearest teleports, and live extras like this week's circus location. Custom timers cover herb runs and everything else without a fixed reset.

Loadout Advisor

An Alt1 app for RuneScape 3. Type your RSN once and pick a combat style or a gathering skill: it works out the best gear your account can wear right now, slot by slot with Grand Exchange prices, then lists the upgrades you're locked out of only by a quest or a one-off unlock — never by levels, because that's just training. Covers melee, ranged, magic and necromancy, the tools for Mining, Woodcutting and Archaeology, and the slayer helmet line.

Prototyper

An Alt1 app for RuneScape 3's Invention skill. Discovering a blueprint means dragging five modules into a hidden "perfect" order with only a vague rating to go on. Prototyper watches the module row and the rating, works out which orders are still possible, and boxes the two modules to swap next, right on top of the game — about 7 swaps to Perfect on average instead of 26 by trial and error.

RuneApps World Map Legend

The teleport markers on the RuneApps RS3 world map only show a menu option number when you hover them, which makes a Digsite pendant and a Skills necklace impossible to tell apart. A searchable key to all 107 icons, plus a Tampermonkey script that rewrites every marker's tooltip to say what it actually is.
